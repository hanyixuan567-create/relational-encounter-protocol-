The legal category of the "pet", and with it the institution of animal ownership, is abolished. No being may henceforth be held as property.

ABOUT

Relational Encounter Protocol is a speculative design project set after a fictional Pet Identity Abolition Act, when society can no longer define human–animal relations through ownership.

The project proposes a civic protocol that observes how two beings actually behave in a shared space, then translates those behaviours — distance, approach, avoidance, following, synchrony, dwelling — into a co-generated visual identity: a Relational Diffraction.

It asks whether a relation, rather than an individual or a deed of title, might be the proper unit of recognition — and what it means when a non-human's consent, inferred from behaviour, is allowed to be binding.
